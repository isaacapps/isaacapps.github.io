Squishtown Privacy Policy

Effective: October 28, 2026 · Operator: Isaac Apps · Contact: isaacapps.kr@gmail.com

1. About this policy

Squishtown (the "app") is a free squishy-making game for Android published by Isaac Apps ("we"), an individual developer based in the Republic of Korea. The app has no accounts and no sign-in, and we do not run a server that receives your game data. This policy explains what the app handles, who else receives information when you use it, and the choices you have.

2. What the app handles

  • Your game progress (your creations and the names you type for them, bottles, pet, coins, level and settings) is saved on your device only. We do not receive it.
  • Age screen. On first launch the app asks for the year you were born. It works out an age range (under 13, 13–17, or 18 and older) and keeps only that range on your device. The year you entered is discarded right away and is never stored or sent anywhere. The age range is used only to choose the ad settings described below and to decide whether optional features are offered. If the stored range is lost (for example after you clear the app's data or reinstall), the app asks again, and no ads are requested until you answer.
  • Ads. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which also passes some ad requests to Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) when Unity offers a better ad (this is called mediation). You see a banner at the bottom of most screens after your first creation, a full-screen ad after some creations (about every third), and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch to double your coins or to unlock a special color or decoration. How ads work depends on the age range from the age screen:
    • Under 13: Google's child-directed ad setting, ads limited to a "G" content rating, non-personalized ads only, and your Android advertising ID is not sent. Unity Ads is told the same and shows only non-personalized (contextual) ads.
    • 13–17: Google's teen ad setting, ads limited to "PG", non-personalized ads only. Unity Ads is told not to personalize ads.
    • 18 and older: the app allows ads up to the "MA" (mature) content rating; Google may apply a lower limit under its family policies for apps that children also use. These ads may be personalized. Google and Unity may use your Android advertising ID and information about your app use to show ads that match your interests. Where the law requires consent (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), ads are personalized only if you agree in Google's consent message; otherwise you get non-personalized ads.
    The consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) is shown where required; your choice is stored on your device and you can change it under Settings > Privacy options when that option is shown. You can also stop personalized ads at any time by deleting or resetting your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). When an ad is requested, the ad software automatically receives technical information from your device: your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), ad and app-interaction events, diagnostic data, an app-set identifier, and, for players 18 and older, the advertising ID. Google and Unity use it to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, as described in Google's Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.
  • Ad settings file. To adjust how often ads appear without releasing an update, the app may download a small settings file (a few ad-frequency values) from a web address we control. The request does not include your age, any device identifier or cookies. As with any web request, the web host that serves the file can see your IP address.
  • Backup. If Android backup is turned on, your device may copy the game's save folder to your Google Account (Google Drive). We cannot access it, and you control it in your Android system settings. The age range, ad and consent settings and identifiers are not part of the backup, so after restoring on a new device the app asks for your birth year again.
  • Play Games (optional, only for players 13 and older, only where offered in your version). If you choose "Connect Play Games" in Settings, Google Play Games Services handles your Play Games profile and cloud save under Google's terms; the app can read your player profile and your cloud save for this game only. Nothing from Play Games is started until you tap that option. You can disconnect in Settings and manage or delete your Play Games data in your Google Account.
  • What the app does not use. There are no in-app purchases or payments in this version. The app does not use Unity Analytics or other Unity analytics services. It does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos or files. Its permissions are network access, vibration and the advertising ID permission (used only for players 18 and older, as described above).

4. Children

The app is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. A player who enters an age under 13 can still play, and gets the most protective settings: child-directed and non-personalized ads limited to a "G" rating from both Google and Unity Ads, no advertising ID, and no Play Games connection. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has used the app in a way that does not fit this policy, write to isaacapps.kr@gmail.com.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Game progress and the age range stay on your device until you clear the app's data (Android Settings > Apps > Squishtown > Storage > Clear data) or uninstall the app.
  • If a Google backup exists, you can delete it in your Google Account or Android backup settings.
  • Data held by Google (ads, consent, Play Games) and Unity (ads) is kept under their policies. You can manage ad settings in your Google Account and Play Games data in your Play Games profile.
  • We hold no account or game data about you, so there is nothing on our side to delete. If you want to ask us anything about your data, or ask us to delete something you believe we hold, write to isaacapps.kr@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the UK or EU under GDPR, Australia under the Privacy Act 1988, the Republic of Korea under the Personal Information Protection Act, or US state laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal information, and to withdraw consent (including consent to personalized ads, in Settings > Privacy options or by resetting your advertising ID). Because we do not hold your data, most requests are handled in your device settings, your Google Account or the consent options in the app; for anything else, contact us. UK and EU users can also complain to their data protection authority, and Australian users can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

7. Security

Network traffic between the app's ad and Play Games components and Google or Unity uses encryption (TLS). Because the game does not send your progress to us, it is stored only where you keep your device and your Google backup.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page and, for changes that affect what the app handles, tell you in the app or on this page before they take effect.

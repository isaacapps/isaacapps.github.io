1. About this app

Read It Again (the "App") lets a parent keep a record of the picture books they have read to their children. You can use every feature without signing in. Signing in is optional and only backs up your records to a server. The App is for adults (18 and older). It has no screen for children to use.

2. What the App processes

These stay on your device and are not sent to us: cover photos (the App copies a photo into the App at a reduced size and the location data embedded in the photo is not kept), your child's nickname, the notes you write while reading together, brag-card photos and lines, and reminder settings. If you do not sign in, all of your records, including your books and reading history, stay on your device.

The App does not ask for your child's name, birthday or photo, and does not ask for your name, profile photo or contacts. Book nicknames and library names are text you type yourself and, if you sign in, they are saved on the server too. Please do not use your child's real name in them.

3. Advertising and your choices

  • The App shows banner ads served by Google AdMob, only below the record list and review screens, never while you are recording. Ad content is limited to a mature-audience rating set by us.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the UK, EEA and Switzerland) the App asks for your consent before requesting ads, and you can change your choice later in the App's ad privacy choices in Settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time: Android Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Google > Ads) > Delete advertising ID. After that, ads are not personalised with that ID.
  • Some US state laws treat sharing an advertising ID for personalised advertising as "sharing" or "targeted advertising". You can opt out by deleting your advertising ID as above.
  • We do not respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals because the App has no web tracking. We do not track you across other apps or websites ourselves; Google may do so through AdMob under Google's own policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. How long we keep it

Information on your device stays until you clear it or uninstall the App. Backup records are kept until you delete your account; if you do not sign in for two years, we delete the account and its server records. Book-lookup requests are not stored. Advertising and Firebase data are kept by Google under its own retention periods.

5. Your rights and how to use them

  • Delete records on your device: delete books and entries in the App, or Android Settings > Apps > Read It Again > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall the App.
  • Delete your account and server records: in the App, Settings > the account row > Delete account and server records. If you have already uninstalled the App, follow isaacapps.github.io/delete.html or email us from the address you signed in with. We delete within 7 days and tell you (see section 9 for statutory time limits). Records on your device are not deleted by this.
  • Access, correct, or get a copy of your information: your records are shown in the App; email us for a copy of anything else we hold on the server.
  • Permissions: you can turn camera and notifications off in Android settings. Turning the camera off stops only taking cover photos and scanning barcodes; turning notifications off stops only return-date reminders. The rest keeps working.
  • We will not treat you differently for using these rights.
  • Backup files you create yourself are saved where you choose and are not sent to us. They are unencrypted zip files, so keep them somewhere you trust. You can leave out your child's nickname and notes when you create one.

6. Security

Data leaves the device over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Only the fields listed above go to our server; photos, your child's nickname, reading notes, advertising ID, device ID and location do not. Server records can only be read and written by the signed-in owner, and we access them with our own account protected by extra verification.

7. Children

The App is for adults aged 18 and over (parents). It has no screen for children to use. We do not ask for a child's name, birthday or photo, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Your child's nickname and your reading notes stay on your device. If you type a child's name into a book nickname or library name and sign in, it is saved on the server, so please use a nickname instead. If we learn that a child under 13 has signed in, we delete that account and its records. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

8. Where information is processed

We are in the Republic of Korea. Backup records are stored in Google's Seoul data centre. Sign-in is processed by Google Firebase in the United States. Cloudflare and Google (for ads, remote settings and ML Kit) process data in other countries, including the United States. Book lookups go from your device directly to Open Library (Internet Archive, USA) and, if needed, Google Books (Google LLC, USA). We rely on these providers' own safeguards for transfers.

1. About this app

Can We Walk? (the "App") tells dog owners whether the weather is good for a walk. You can use every feature without signing in. Signing in is optional and only backs up your records. The App is for adults (18 and older) who look after dogs.

2. What the App processes

Your dog's name, birthday, breed, look and health settings, photos, walk records (stamps, notes, diary lines), outfits, reminder times and the neighbourhood you pick are stored on your device. When you add a photo from your gallery or camera, the App copies it into the App and removes the location data embedded in the photo.

3. Advertising and your choices

  • The App shows ads served by Google AdMob. Where the law requires it (for example in the UK, EEA and Switzerland) the App asks for your consent before requesting ads, and you can change your choice later in Settings > Privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time: Android Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Google > Ads) > Delete advertising ID. After that, ads are not personalised with that ID.
  • Some US state laws treat sharing an advertising ID for personalised advertising as "sharing" or "targeted advertising". You can opt out in the App's Privacy choices screen or by deleting your advertising ID as above.
  • We do not respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals because the App has no web tracking. We do not track you across other apps or websites ourselves; Google may do so through AdMob under Google's own policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. How long we keep it

Information on your device stays until you clear it or uninstall the App. Weather requests are not logged. Backup records are kept while your account exists; if you do not sign in for two years, we delete the account. Advertising and Firebase data are kept by Google under its own retention periods.

5. Your rights and how to use them

  • Delete everything on your device: use the delete-data option in the App's Settings, or Android Settings > Apps > Can We Walk? > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall the App.
  • Delete your account and backup: Settings > the sign-in row > Delete account. Or visit isaacapps.github.io/delete.html, or email us from the address you signed in with. We reply within 7 days (see section 9 for statutory time limits).
  • Access, correct, or get a copy of your information: your records are shown in the App; email us for a copy of anything else we hold.
  • Permissions: you can turn location, camera and notifications off in Android settings. The related feature stops and the rest keeps working.
  • We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

6. Security

Data leaves the device over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Sign-in and backup send only the fields that are needed. Backup files you export are stored on your device without encryption; please keep them safe.

7. Children

The App is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

8. Where information is processed

We are in the Republic of Korea. Cloudflare, Google and (for forecasts) MET Norway process data in other countries, including the United States. We rely on these providers' own safeguards for transfers.
